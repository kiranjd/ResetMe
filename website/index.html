Why look at work patterns?

Task transitions can carry a cost. Sophie Leroy’s experiments found that attention can remain on unfinished work after a switch, affecting performance on the next task. Leroy, 2009 ↗

In a study of interrupted work, participants worked faster but reported more stress, frustration and effort. Mark, Gudith & Klocke, 2008 ↗

These studies motivate reflection; they do not validate ResetMe as a measure of focus. The app shows activity patterns, not productivity, stress or recovery scores.
