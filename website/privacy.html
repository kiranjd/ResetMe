Website privacy

We count homepage views and clicks on Download for Mac to understand whether people find ResetMe useful.

Our Cloudflare collector stores daily totals by event, referring website hostname and country. It does not store IP addresses, full referring URLs, query strings, visitor identifiers or individual event records. We use no analytics cookies or browser storage. Cloudflare processes requests to provide the service; GitHub hosts this website.

These are counts of views and clicks, not unique people or confirmed installations. Repeat visits count again. Browser privacy tools may block measurement.

We respect browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals. You can also open the homepage without analytics. That option applies to this page load. This website tracking does not add telemetry to the ResetMe Mac app.
